My Story

From the block to the boardroom

I didn't start in boardrooms. I started on retail floors — and I've been climbing ever since.

Born and raised in Miami, I learned early how to show up, serve people, and outwork the room. My first jobs were in retail and e-commerce operations, where I picked up the discipline of high-volume, get-it-done work.

From there I grew into managing client accounts at a marketing agency, leading community and youth-engagement work, and stepping into banking — where I help people reach their financial goals every day. Along the way I built my own things: a streetwear brand and marketplace I grew to thousands of followers, and a consulting firm helping young, first-generation entrepreneurs become fundable. My work with youth in Miami Gardens even landed me on WSVN 7News.

Now, finishing my degree at Full Sail, I bring all of it — the discipline of banking, the creativity of brand strategy, and the drive of an entrepreneur — to whatever I take on next.
